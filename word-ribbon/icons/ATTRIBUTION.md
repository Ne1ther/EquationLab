# Ribbon artwork

The current artwork is drawn for Equation Lab and distributed under the repository's MIT license. Its font-independent geometry lives in [`../icon-artwork.mjs`](../icon-artwork.mjs), with inspectable SVG exports in [`svg/`](svg/). Run `node word-ribbon/build-icons.mjs` to regenerate transparent 16, 32 and 80 pixel PNGs. Small icons use a stronger optical weight; large native Ribbon buttons embed the 80 pixel variant for Retina displays.
